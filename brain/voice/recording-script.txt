VOICE RECORDING SCRIPT - TheLittleEpic (read once, about 4 minutes)
Tips: quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, your normal "telling a friend something surprising" voice.
Pause at every full stop. If you make a mistake, pause and read the sentence again. Don't stop the recording.

PART 1 - Warm up (just be yourself)
Hey, it's me. I'm recording this so every video sounds exactly like my own voice.
Today is a good day. The coffee is hot, the sun is out, and I've got a lot of ideas.

PART 2 - Storyteller (calm, like the start of a video)
What if you played a game for thirty days straight? No sleep. No breaks.
Picture this. It's two in the morning. The room is dark. Only the screen is glowing.
Remy grabs the controller, takes a deep breath, and presses start.
In nineteen sixty four, a teenager in San Diego tried to stay awake for eleven days.
Nobody expected what happened next.

PART 3 - Surprise and excitement (more energy)
Wait. That can't be right!
One point three million alligators. In one state!
Forty percent? Seriously? That's almost half!
And that's exactly why the winner usually loses.

PART 4 - Questions (let your voice go up)
Would you still move there?
How long have you ever stayed awake?
What would you do first?
Did you see that coming?

PART 5 - Calm and serious (slower, lower)
Here's the real rule. Watch the sky, not just the water.
In the game, you respawn. In real life, you only get one save file.
Take a moment. Think about it. Then decide.

PART 6 - Numbers and dates (clear and steady)
Zero, one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten.
Seventeen hours. Twenty four hours. Two hundred sixty four hours.
Fifty kilometers an hour. Thirty percent. A hundred and fifty songs.
Monday, Wednesday, Friday. January, June, November.
Nineteen sixty eight. Two thousand. Twenty twenty six.

PART 7 - Sounds of English (read naturally, these cover every sound)
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
She sells sea shells by the sea shore, but the sharp chef chose cheese.
Thick fog rolled through the valley while the jazz music played on.
A big black bug bit a big black bear, and the bear bled blue blood.
Please measure the treasure, then choose the usual vision.
Why would you watch the weather when the world is waiting?
Bright lights, loud thunder, and a long, slow, quiet ride home.

PART 8 - Free talk (about one minute, say anything)
Talk about your day, a game you love, or a video idea. Laugh if something is funny.

PART 9 - Sign off
And that's it. Thanks for watching. See you in the next one.
