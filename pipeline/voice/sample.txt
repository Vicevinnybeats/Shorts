Every story on this channel starts with a small, true thing. A number that sounds impossible. A place you have walked past a thousand times. A creature you never once looked at closely. Tonight, we slow the world right down. The kitchen light hums. The clock on the wall ticks forty times before anyone notices. Somewhere under the table, something tiny is about to make the bravest decision of its life. It does not know the floor is a desert. It does not know the crumbs are mountains. It only knows that home is on the other side, and the night is getting colder. So it takes one step. Then another. And for the first time, we follow it all the way. Because the smallest journeys are often the longest ones. Stay until the end, and tell me: what tiny thing would you follow home?
