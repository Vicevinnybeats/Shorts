# WHAT IF #4: What if you healed like a GTA 6 character? (script + fact-check 2026-11-26, owner approved the title)
- Minor cuts close in about 7-10 days (minor cuts/scrapes); remodelling/maturation continues 21 days to a year or more.
  https://www.healthpartners.com/blog/wound-healing-stages-how-to-tell-if-a-wound-is-healing-infected-or-chronic/ ,
  https://www.cedars-sinai.org/health-topics/how-wounds-heal , https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000741.htm
- Healed scar ~80% as strong as original skin (plastic surgeon quoted by Marshfield Clinic; KidsHealth: almost 80-90%).
  "about eighty percent" used. https://shine365.marshfieldclinic.org/wellness/scars , https://kidshealth.org/en/kids/scars.html
- After whole-blood donation: plasma replaced in ~24 hours; red cells ~4-6 weeks; American Red Cross requires at least
  8 weeks (56 days) between whole-blood donations. https://redcrossblood.org/faq.html ,
  https://www.blood.co.uk/the-donation-process/after-your-donation/how-your-body-replaces-blood/ ,
  https://stanfordbloodcenter.org/pulse-spring23-what-really-happens-to-your-body-after-you-donate-blood-effects-risks-and-recovery/
- Minor fractures heal in about 6-8 weeks; severe fractures 3-6 months (NHS Sherwood Forest Hospitals fracture leaflet).
  https://www.sfh-tr.nhs.uk/media/16693/pil202308-01-fra-fractures.pdf , https://theros.org.uk/information-and-support/osteoporosis/living-with-osteoporosis/recovering-from-a-broken-bone/
