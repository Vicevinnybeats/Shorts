# WHAT IF #1: What if you played GTA 6 for 30 days straight? (new "What if" format, 2026-11-21)
- Williamson & Feyer (2000), Occup Environ Med 57:649-655: after 17-19 h awake, performance on SOME tests equal to or
  worse than BAC 0.05% (the limit in many countries); responses up to 50% slower. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1739867
- Randy Gardner, 17, San Diego, stayed awake 264 hours (~11 days), ending 8 Jan 1964 (high-school science project;
  monitored later by William Dement). Effects: from day 2 trouble with tongue twisters/words; day 3 nausea, memory
  trouble; later moodiness, poor concentration, short-term memory loss; paranoia/hallucinations reported in secondary
  sources ("some reports say"). Slept ~14 h afterwards. Guinness stopped accepting attempts (safety).
  https://www.npr.org/2024/01/28/1227217274/sleep-deprivation-record , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Gardner_(record_holder)
- Microsleeps: brief involuntary sleep episodes of seconds during sleep deprivation (general sleep science; NHLBI/NIH).
- The day 2 / day 3 beats follow Gardner's account; the "car drifting" line is a fictional game moment.
- No game footage/logos; "GTA 6" only as words. Choice cards: "WHERE DO YOU CRASH FIRST? A) HOUR 17 B) DAY 2 C) DAY 3 D) NEVER".
