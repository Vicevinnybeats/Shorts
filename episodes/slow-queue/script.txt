Maya picked the shortest queue. She still lost.
Saturday. Three checkouts. Maya counts the carts, picks the shortest line, and smiles at her brother Sam in the line next to her.
Then it happens. A coupon. A price check. A card that will not work.
Sam's line flows. Maya's line freezes.
It feels like a curse. It is just math.
With three lines, the chance that yours is the fastest is only one in three. Two times out of three, another line wins. Add more checkouts, and the odds get even worse.
And one slow customer can freeze a whole line, while every other line keeps moving.
That is why some shops use one long snake line that feeds every till. A slow customer only blocks one till, and the line simply flows to the others.
Nobody gets unlucky. Everybody waits about the same.
Sam reaches the till first. He waves.
Maya switches lines. Her old line starts moving.
Have you ever switched lines and instantly regretted it?
