Kai just broke the golden rule of haggling. And he's about to win.
Saturday flea market. Everyone wants a bargain.
Kai's old guitar has a blank tag. Rosa picks it up.
Kai speaks first. Two hundred dollars.
Rosa nearly drops it. Two hundred?
Wait. Everyone says never name the price first. Why did it work?
In negotiation experiments, whoever made the first offer tended to get the better deal.
That first number becomes an anchor. Counteroffers get dragged toward it.
Even a random wheel does it. In one test, people who saw ten guessed twenty five percent. Those who saw sixty five guessed forty five.
The final price follows it. Just know the real value. Go too wild, and buyers may walk.
Rosa counters with one hundred. They meet at one sixty.
Deal. Rosa still thinks she won.
Next Saturday, Rosa runs her own stall. She names her price first.
So, would you say the price first?
