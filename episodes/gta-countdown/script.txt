Kai has waited thirteen years for this game. And science says the best part is already over.
Two days to go. A countdown on his wall. A launch-night plan.
His sister Rosa doesn't get it. Why does a game you haven't played make you this happy?
Wait. That's the strange part. The happy bit might be the waiting itself.
Dutch researchers followed fifteen hundred adults around their holidays.
People with a trip coming up were happier than everyone else. Before they left.
After they came home? Mostly no happier at all. Even a very relaxed trip's boost was gone after about eight weeks.
Looking forward is a free reward. You enjoy the thing again and again, before it even happens.
Like the smell of cake in the oven. Sometimes better than the first bite.
So Kai has a choice. Rush it in one weekend. Or keep something to look forward to.
Launch night. One mission. Then he stops.
So, will you finish it in a week, or save the best for later?
