What if you moved to the real Leonida? GTA 6's map is a made up Florida. So let's pack a bag and try the real one.
Day one. Remy lands in Miami. The air feels like a warm, wet towel.
He wants the full game experience. Swamps. Beaches. Fast cars.
Day three. A golf course. Something is lying next to the pond. It's not a log.
Florida has around one point three million alligators. Wildlife officials say they live in all sixty seven counties.
Scared? Here's the twist. Serious alligator injuries are rare. Most gators avoid people.
The bigger danger is above you.
Day ten. Every afternoon, the same sky. Sunshine at noon. Thunder by four.
Florida gets more lightning per square kilometer than any other state, and it has led the country in lightning deaths for years.
Day thirty. A cone appears on every TV. Hurricane season.
Roughly forty percent of all hurricanes that hit the United States make landfall in Florida.
So Remy learns the real rules. Watch the sky, not just the water. When thunder roars, go indoors.
In the game, you respawn. In real life, the weather doesn't care.
