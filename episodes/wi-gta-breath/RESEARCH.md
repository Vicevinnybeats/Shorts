# WHAT IF #6: What if you held your breath like a GTA 6 character? (script + fact-check 2026-11-27, owner approved title)
- Untrained adults: about 30-90 s (most 45-60 s); trained freedivers several minutes.
  https://www.healthline.com/health/holding-your-breath , https://www.sciencefocus.com/the-human-body/how-long-can-you-hold-breath
- Static apnea record without pure oxygen (AIDA): 11:35, Stephane Mifsud ("over eleven minutes"). With oxygen
  pre-breathing (Guinness): 24:37, Budimir Sobat (not used). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Static_apnea
- Cold water shock: involuntary gasp in the first minute; 1-10-1 rule (Gordon Giesbrecht / Cold Water Boot Camp):
  1 min to control breathing, ~10 min meaningful movement, ~1 h before hypothermia unconsciousness. Memory aid, not exact.
  https://csbc.ca/1-10-1-principle/ , https://www.army.mil/article/212474/tips_to_survive_a_fall_into_cold_water ,
  https://parks.wa.gov/about/news-center/field-guide-blog/cold-water-shock-what-it-and-how-protect-yourself
- Hypoxic ("shallow water") blackout: breath-holding/hyperventilating underwater can cause blackout without warning;
  never swim alone, never ignore the urge to breathe (Red Cross / YMCA / USA Swimming joint statement; CDC).
  https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/articles/shallow-water-hypoxic-blackout , https://www.cdc.gov/drowning/prevention/
- Show no drowning; Remy always safe (buddy, life jacket). Safety message in metadata. No game footage/logos.
