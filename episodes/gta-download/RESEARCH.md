# Why the GTA 6 Download Feels Endless (scripted 2026-11-17, not built)
- Harrison, Amento, Kuznetsov & Bell (2007), "Rethinking the progress bar", UIST '07 pp.115-118: same-duration
  progress bars feel faster or slower depending on their behaviour; accelerating toward the end feels faster,
  slowing at the end feels slower. https://www.chrisharrison.net/index.php/Research/ProgressBars
- Time-left estimates = remaining bytes / current (or recent) speed, so they jump when speed changes (general engineering fact).
- GTA 6 digital pre-orders can pre-load before launch (so "launch night download" is framed as a fiction moment).
- Kettle line is an everyday comparison (attention makes time feel longer), not a claimed study.
