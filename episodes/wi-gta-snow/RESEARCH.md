# GTA 6 #7: What if it snowed in Vice City? (2026-11-27, owner approved)
- 19 Jan 1977 cold wave: snow flurries in South Florida (Broward/Dade), ~8-9:30 am, a trace; the only known trace of snow
  ever reported in the greater Miami area; not recorded at Miami Intl Airport (official site), reported at Hollywood
  and Everglades NP stations. https://www.weather.gov/media/mfl/news/SnowSouthFlorida45th.pdf ,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_wave_of_January_1977 , https://wsvn.com/news/local/miami-dade/miami-dade-broward-mark-43rd-anniversary-of-snowfall/
  Script says "gone by mid morning" and "the only snow ever reported around Miami".
- Green iguanas become cold-stunned and can fall from trees when it gets cold (~low 40s to 50 F / ~5-10 C); usually
  recover when it warms. No exact threshold in the script ("cold enough"). https://www.livescience.com/animals/reptiles/why-do-iguanas-fall-from-trees-in-florida ,
  https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/article/frozen-iguanas-falling-florida-freeze ("most" recover - hedged).
- Manatees need water warmer than 68 F (20 C) in prolonged cold; gather at springs and power plant warm-water outflows
  (FWC). https://www.wusf.org/environment/2025-01-19/manatees-congregate-warm-waters-power-plants-winter-storms-graze-florida ,
  https://savethemanatee.org/a-warm-water-action-plan-for-manatees/
