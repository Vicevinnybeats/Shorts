Day one. The kitchen floor is thirteen hundred times longer than Pip.
Pip is an odorous house ant, three millimetres long, and somewhere across this tiled desert is the smell of sugar.
No map. No trail. Just a sweet signal in the air.
Tile one. Tile two. A crumb the size of a boulder. A table leg like a tower.
Then the ground shakes. A slipper lands so close, the wind knocks her flat.
She gets up.
And there it is. One grain of spilled sugar, glittering like a diamond.
Ants can lift many times their own body weight, so Pip carries it alone.
But here is the clever part. All the way home, she touches the tip of her body to the floor, painting an invisible chemical road.
By morning, her sisters are marching on it.
One ant found the way. The whole colony followed.
What tiny thing in your house would make the best adventure?
