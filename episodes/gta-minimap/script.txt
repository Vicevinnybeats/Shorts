Two friends. Same new map. Only one of them can find his way home.
GTA 6 is out. A whole new state to explore: Leonida, a made-up Florida.
Max follows the arrow on his minimap. Turn left. Turn right. Arrived.
Ava turns the minimap off. She gets lost. She finds the beach, the bridge, a pink hotel.
One week later, the minimap breaks. Max is lost on his own street. Ava drives straight home.
Wait. Max drove more. Why does Ava know the way?
Because following an arrow isn't the same as learning a place.
Researchers in Montreal tested regular drivers on finding their way in a virtual town.
People who used GPS more had worse spatial memory when the GPS was gone.
And in a small three-year follow-up, more GPS meant a steeper drop.
Landmarks build a map in your head. The arrow lets that part sleep.
It's like copying homework. The page is full. Your head isn't.
So, minimap on or off?
