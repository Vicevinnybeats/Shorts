# WHAT IF #5: What if you had GTA 6 money in real life? (script + fact-check 2026-11-27, owner approved title)
- A US banknote weighs about 1 gram, any denomination (BEP fun facts, archived; Wikipedia $100 bill ~1.0 g).
  $1,000,000 in $100 bills = 10,000 bills = ~10 kg (~22 lb).
  https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20090114222615/http:/www.bep.treas.gov/document.cfm/18/106 ,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_one-hundred-dollar_bill
- Note thickness 0.0043 in (0.11 mm) (BEP/MoneyFactory, archived) -> 10,000 notes ~1.1 m uncompressed ("about a meter").
  https://webharvest.gov/peth04/20041015012917/http://www.moneyfactory.com/section.cfm/19
- Counting 1 bill/second: 10,000 s = 2.8 h ("almost three hours") - arithmetic.
- Banks must file a Currency Transaction Report (FinCEN Form 112) for cash transactions over $10,000 in a business day
  (Bank Secrecy Act, 31 CFR 1010.311); structuring deposits to evade the report is illegal (31 U.S.C. 5324).
  https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/shared/CTRPamphlet.pdf , https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/bank-secrecy-act
