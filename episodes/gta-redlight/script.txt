He can do anything in GTA 6. And he still stops at every red light.
Launch week. Kai finally gets the controller. Fast car. Empty road. Red light. He stops.
Nia stares. Nobody's chasing you. Go! He waits for green anyway.
And he's not alone. One PC Gamer writer posted: Help, I can't stop obeying traffic laws in GTA 6.
Wait. It's a game. So why won't his hands break the rules?
Because a lot of what you do isn't a choice. It's a habit.
Psychologists had students write down what they were doing, hour by hour.
In one study, about forty three percent of their actions were habits. Done almost every day, in the same place, often while thinking about something else.
A habit is a shortcut. The cue appears, the action follows. Red light, foot on the brake.
It's like typing your password without thinking. Your fingers know it. You don't.
Next morning, Nia moves the cereal to a new shelf. Kai opens the old cupboard. Three times.
