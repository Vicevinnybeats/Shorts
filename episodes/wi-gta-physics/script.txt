Test one. Remy climbs to the roof. In the game, this is a shortcut.
In real life, gravity speeds you up every second.

Test two. A car. In the game, you crash at full speed, climb out, and keep driving.
Here's the real math. A crash at just fifty kilometers an hour hits your body like falling from the third floor of a building.

That's why seat belts matter. They spread the stop across your hips and chest, instead of the windshield.
Test three. Walking. In the game, a car bumps you and you get right back up.
In real life, more than ninety five percent of people hit by a car at thirty kilometers an hour survive. At fifty, about eighty five percent.
That's why many towns put thirty zones near schools.
