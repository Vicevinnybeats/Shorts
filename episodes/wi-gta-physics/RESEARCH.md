# WHAT IF #3: What if GTA physics worked in real life? (script 2026-11-21, build after #2)
- Terminal velocity belly-to-earth skydiver ~53-56 m/s (~190-200 km/h, ~120 mph), reached after roughly 12 s of free
  fall (commonly cited; "about"). https://hypertextbook.com/facts/1998/JianHuang.shtml , https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/sports-and-leisure/mathematics-skydiving
- 50 km/h crash ~ falling from the third floor (Victoria, Australia Road to Zero exhibit; French officials say 4 storeys;
  physics: 13.9 m/s ~ 10 m drop). https://www.roadtozero.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/392477/Background-Crash-Forces-Elevator_v06.pdf
- Kinetic energy ~ v^2: double speed -> 4x energy (physics).
- Pedestrians: >95% survive at 30 km/h impact, ~85% at 50 km/h (SWOV overview). https://www.swov.nl/en/node/16371 ,
  https://road-safety.transport.ec.europa.eu/statistics-and-analysis/statistics-and-analysis-archive/pedestrians/no-speed-no-mass-and-no-protection_en
- Seat belts spread deceleration over pelvis/chest (standard road-safety explanation). 30 km/h zones near schools: common in many countries.
- Never show realistic gore; neon line-art only; no game footage/logos.
